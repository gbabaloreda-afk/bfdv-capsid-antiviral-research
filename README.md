Uncovering Potential Antiviral Targets in the Beak and Feather Disease Virus Capsid
Supporting research materials for a computational investigation of the beak and feather disease virus (BFDV) capsid.

Babalo Reda
Independent researcher

Research materials
The downloadable research archive contains available sequence data, historical analysis records, structural verification, figure source data, analysis code, reconstructed cavity outputs and publication figures.

The complete archive is attached to the repository's Releases section.

Evidence and reproducibility
Historical analyses and subsequent reconstructions are documented separately.

The sequence collection contains 519 records representing 368 unique amino-acid sequences.
All 28 historical candidate residue identities matched the observed structural reference in the saved comparison.
This structural comparison does not independently reproduce the original sequence-alignment conservation frequencies.
Reconstructed cavities contain 73 alpha-sphere centres each, rather than the 78 reported historically.
Recovered notebook evidence includes 30 individual docking seed scores and summaries of docking, pose reproducibility and geometric clearance.
Original molecular inputs and complete pairwise pose records were not recovered by the notebook extraction.
Docking predictions do not establish experimental binding or antiviral activity.
